Taking Action

Creating and implementing our solution

The Product

Our project included creating a presentation about getting healthy sleep and how important getting healthy sleep is. The presentation explained the importance of sleep, the effects of poor sleep and activities to get better sleep. We made the audience perform these activities to ensure that our teachings were meaningful.

How Our Project Addresses the Community Need

Our presentation spread awareness about sleep by educating the audience about how sleep affects the physical and mental health of our community. Through researched information, statistics and graphs, the presentation helped the audience understand the importance of sleep and helped them understand the consequences of unhealthy sleep. The presentation helps the audience understand what activities can help get better sleep and what activities affect the quality of their sleep. The presentation encourages the people to understand about healthy sleep and make a positive change in their lives to have a healthier lifestyle.

Challenges We Faced

One challenge we faced was organizing our research and selecting the most important information to include in the presentation. There was a lot of information about sleep, so we had to carefully choose what would be most useful for our audience. We overcame this by dividing tasks among group members and discussing which points were the most relevant. This helped us create a clear and informative presentation.
